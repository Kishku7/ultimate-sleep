# Modrinth 'Sleep' Mods - Research List

Source: Modrinth search query "Sleep" (project_type:mod). Total returned by API: 249.
Kept after filtering: 180. Excluded: 69.

Exclusions applied: bed/block reskins and sleeping-bag/hammock item mods; mods that only
set sleep percentage or enable one-player/single-player sleep (vanilla minimum); mods
unrelated to player sleep mechanics (pets, decoration, music discs, PC/server/hopper sleep);
villager-only or other non-player-usable; mods that fully remove or disable sleeping.
Uncertain cases were kept.
